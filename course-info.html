Course Info

Everything that is not week-by-week. For what is due and when, use the weekly schedule.

01

Materials

  1. Student Guide — Course overview, learning outcomes and DEIP model. Read in Week 1.
  2. Syllabus — Policies, grading, attendance, academic integrity, AI use. When you need a rule.
  3. Semester Timeline — Every assessment, build, visit and reading by week. Print it in Week 1.
  4. Reading Packet — All 10 readings with glossaries and seminar prompts. Before every reading task.
  5. Reading Task Sheets — The sheet you fill in for each reading. Alongside the reading.
  6. Templates & Tools — Cornell notes, DEIP, Planning Guide, CCD Visit Log. Every week.
  7. Assignment Tables — Every task for all 14 weeks in one printable PDF. Offline backup.
  8. Student Workbook — Eight skills units, used mostly in class. Bring to class.
  9. Speech Rubrics — Requirements and marking criteria for both speeches. Before you plan, not after.
  10. Assessment Information — What each assessment measures and how it is marked. Two weeks before each assessment.
  11. Experiential Learning Tracker — Your CCD record. File → Make a copy first. After every CCD activity.
  12. Week 8 Meeting Prep Sheet — What to bring, self-rating, action planning. Week 8 only.
  13. Portfolio Assignment Guide — Weekly portfolio tasks with dates and checkboxes. Every week.
Where things go. Almost everything is submitted to your Student Folder on the course Google Drive. Label every upload with the week, for example “Week 1 Reading 1 Paragraph”. A few tasks are on paper in class; CCD modules are tracked on Brightspace.

04

Portfolio

Your portfolio has two lives. The Working Portfolio is your evidence bank from Weeks 1 to 13: save work as you go, label it clearly, and do not worry about polish. The Final Showcase Portfolio (due Sun Dec 13, worth 10%) is the curated version: 5 artifacts with captions, a major DEIP reflection, and a closing statement. You present 2–3 of those artifacts at the Student Showcase on Thu Dec 10.

Policies

Attendance

2 free absences, no explanation needed. 3 = participation deduction. 4 = F for participation. 5 = course failure. If you know in advance, notify me before class.

Late work

5% deducted per day. Not accepted after 7 days. Email me before the due date if you have extenuating circumstances.

Deadlines

Weekly tasks are due at 23:59 on the Monday after the week they belong to, unless the task says otherwise. Portfolio builds and CCD logs sometimes fall on a Thursday. Always check the date on the task itself.

AI use

Task-specific. I will tell you when AI is permitted. Submitting AI-generated content as your own is never allowed. When permitted, include an AI disclosure. Full details in the Syllabus.

Office hours

FAQ

Q1Where do I submit my work?

Student Folder on the course Google Drive for almost everything (label with the week). On paper for a few tasks like the Planning Guide. Brightspace for the three CCD modules only. The task always says which.

Q2When exactly is something due?

Open the weekly schedule and check the date chip on the task. General rule: 23:59 Monday after the week. Portfolio builds and CCD logs often fall on a Thursday instead.

Q3How much time should this take?

8–10 hours per week outside class. Your Week 2 time-tracking task gives you a personal baseline.

Q4I missed a class. What now?

Check with a classmate, then open the week on the schedule. Missing class does not extend a deadline. 2 free absences before it affects participation.

Q5Can I use AI?

Sometimes. I will tell you when. Submitting AI-generated content as your own is never allowed. See the Syllabus.

Q6What is DEIP?

Describe → Explain → Interpret → Plan. All four parts, every time. Plan is the one people forget.

Q7What does a listening task include?

Cornell-style notes, 5–10 vocabulary words with meanings, main ideas + supporting details, and a short summary. Template in Templates & Tools.

Q8How do I book office hours?

Walk-in Tuesdays 13:00–14:15, Room N745. Other times: book a slot. Week 8 meeting has a separate link.
