Presentation Guide Quiz: Answer Key

EAP 100: Future Focused · 20 points

Stop. Only open this page after you have finished the quiz. Part A is 1 point per question. Part B is 2 points per question. Equivalent wording is accepted for short answers. This is a comprehension check, not the official presentation rubric.

Part B · 10 points

11 [2] Source types

1 point: A is primary because it presents original survey findings. 1 point: B is secondary because it interprets another source’s research.

12 [2] Arguable claim

Example: “Employers should require human review of AI-screened job applications because automated screening can overlook qualified applicants.” 1 point for a specific actor and proposed action; 1 point for an arguable, relevant reason and a manageable focus. Other defensible claims are acceptable.

13 [2] Persuasive appeal

1 point: logos, because the speaker connects evidence to reasoning. 1 point for a useful ethical improvement, such as citing the original study accurately, explaining the data’s limitations, adding relevant evidence, or addressing a strong counterargument. All three appeals are not required.

14 [2] Peer feedback

1 point: shorten and rehearse the speech so it lasts 4 to 5 minutes, excluding Q&A. 1 point: reduce or combine content to make 3 to 4 content slides; keep references separate. Specific suggestions such as cutting repetition or focusing on two main reasons are welcome.

15 [2] Deadline

1 point: upload outline, slides, and APA references before 11:15 AM, Shanghai time, on Tuesday, November 24, not before the individual speaking turn. 1 point: add a working video link to the Excel spreadsheet in the Final Presentation assessment folder by midnight (23:59) on Tuesday, November 24.
