Discussion Cards

Sixteen cards for small-group discussion. Each one has a main question, a quote with its page number, and three follow-ups. The last three cards mix two readings: good practice for the Listening Exam 2 synthesis task.

Open the readingsPDF Reading Packet

How to use the cards

  1. Groups of 3–4. Take one card. Spend about 10 minutes on it.
  2. Find the page first. Read the quote in the reading before you start talking.
  3. Answer the main question, then work through the follow-ups. You do not need to agree.
  4. Write one takeaway before you take the next card.

Roles: rotate every card

LeaderReads the card aloud and keeps time.
CiterFinds the page and cites the reading aloud.
ChallengerAsks the follow-ups and pushes back.
RecorderWrites the group’s takeaway.

Useful language

CiteAccording to Hyland (2006), …
On page 30, the reading says …
Agree / disagreeI agree, because …
I see it differently. …
Be carefulThis may be true for …, but not …
The evidence shows …, but it cannot prove …
BuildAdding to what ___ said, …
Can you give an example?
