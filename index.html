    🚀 First Week Checklist

    New here? Complete these before your second class (Thu Sep 3), and submit the Week 1 tasks by Mon Sep 7, 23:59.

    1. Watch the Cornell Notes video (3 min, in Week 1 below)
    2. Set up your portfolio in your Student Folder on the course Google Drive
    3. Begin the Career Development Online Course on Brightspace (all 3 modules by Thu Oct 22)
    4. Read Reading 1: What Counts as Work? (in your Reading Packet)
    5. Write your first DEIP reflection (~200 words)
    Why the future of work? The theme is the vehicle; academic English is the destination. As you read, listen, discuss and present your way through it, you build the seven Future-Ready Competencies employers and universities now look for: critical, analytical and creative thinking; self-direction, adaptability and learning-to-learn; collaboration, communication and empathy; ethics, responsibility and citizenship; digital and technological fluency; sustainability and global awareness; and resilience, wellbeing and acting under uncertainty. Your portfolio is where that development becomes visible — to you, and to a future employer.
