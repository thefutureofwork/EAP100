What matters most ★ Key

Questions marked ★ Key matter most. If you are short on time, do these first.

  1. Find the argument: What does Shen want, and what are his three ideas?
  2. Check the evidence: A story shows one person’s experience. It does not prove something is true for everyone.
  3. Give your opinion: Did he persuade you? Why or why not?
  4. Use careful language: Words like may, can and some make claims more accurate.

Short answers are fine. Use your own words. You may disagree with Shen. A confident speaker is not always right.

C. Check the evidence ★ Key

About 15 minutes

Evidence can show something, but not everything. Finish both sentences for each example. You do not need to look for other sources.

C1His Etsy story

This story shows that
But it cannot prove that

It is one person’s story. Would it work for everyone?

C2The 2010 study about graduates’ jobs and degrees

This study shows that
But it cannot prove that

The study is from 2010 and he does not name it. Is it still true today? In every country?
