    🚀 First Week Checklist

    New here? Complete these before your second class (Thu Sep 3), and submit the Week 1 tasks by Mon Sep 7, 23:59.

    4. Read Reading 1: What Counts as Work? (in your Reading Packet)
    5. Write your first DEIP reflection (~200 words)
    Why the future of work? The theme is the vehicle; academic English is the destination. As you read, listen, discuss and present your way through it, you build the seven Future-Ready Competencies employers and universities now look for: critical, analytical and creative thinking; self-direction, adaptability and learning-to-learn; collaboration, communication and empathy; ethics, responsibility and citizenship; digital and technological fluency; sustainability and global awareness; and resilience, wellbeing and acting under uncertainty. Your portfolio is where that development becomes visible — to you, and to a future employer.

    • ❓Ask questions in the moment. Do not wait until the end of class. If something is unclear, raise your hand or speak up right away.
    • 🗣️Check information with your instructor. You are welcome to speak to classmates, but do not assume what they tell you is correct. When in doubt, ask me directly.

    • 📤Everything goes to your Student Folder unless the task says “on paper” or “in class”. Label it with the week, for example “Week 1 Reading 1 Paragraph”.

    FAQ

    Where do I submit my work?

    Upload everything to Your Student Folder on the course Google Drive, using the week label from the assignment (for example “Week 1 Reading 1 Paragraph”). Keep a copy of portfolio-related work in your portfolio. A few tasks are done on paper or in class — each task above says which.

    When is work due?

    Weekly reading, listening and reflection tasks are due at 23:59 on the Monday after the week they belong to, unless the task says otherwise. Portfolio builds and CCD logs sometimes fall earlier in the week, so check the due chip on each task.

    How do I book office hours?

    Walk-in: Tuesdays, 13:00–14:15, Room N745, first come first served. By appointment: book a slot at least 24 hours in advance for other times. Your Week 8 individual meeting uses a separate booking link — do not use the walk-in hour for it.

    What if I miss a class?

    You have 2 free absences (no explanation needed). For each absence, check with a classmate about what was covered. 3 absences = participation deduction. 4 = F for participation. 5 = course failure. If you know in advance, notify me before class.

    What if my assignment is late?

    5% deducted per day late. Not accepted after 7 days. If you have extenuating circumstances, email me before the due date. An absence does not excuse a late submission.

    Can I use AI tools for assignments?
