Before you start

  1. Do the pre-listening tasks in Tools and Templates first. Review the key terms, predict what the speaker will argue, and note what you already know about the topic.
  2. Read every question before you press play. Underline the key words so you know exactly what to listen for.
  3. Set up your Cornell notes page. Write your questions and predictions in the left column now. Fill in answers as you listen.
  4. Print or open the blank answer sheet and set aside 70 uninterrupted minutes.
  5. Play the talk once only. No pause, rewind, or second viewing.
  6. Complete the paper under exam conditions.
Today's focus: prepare before you listen. Strong listeners arrive with questions already written. Practise catching the speaker's main claim, recording numbers and examples accurately, and building a genuine agreement/tension connection in Section C.

After you finish

  2. Check your work against the exemplar and mark scheme.
