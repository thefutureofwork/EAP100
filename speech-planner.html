1. Claim and audience ★ Key

1aMy claim

Formula: [Who] should [do what] because [main reason].
Example: NYU Shanghai should require a first-year AI literacy module because students need to evaluate AI output in every field.

1bMy audience, and what I want them to do

1cClaim check

Swap planners. Your partner reads your claim and ticks each box that is true. Then swap back.

  • A reasonable person could disagree with it.
  • It names who should do what.
  • It connects to the future of work.
  • I can explain it clearly in 4–5 minutes.
All 4 ticked? Skip 1d. Go to 1b.1 box empty? Fix only that row in 1d.2+ empty, or no claim? Do all of 1d.

1dClaim Doctor: fix the box you missed

Find the box your partner did not tick. Read the problem, apply the fix, and look at the example. Then write your new claim below.

Box missedThe problemThe fixBefore → after
Could disagreeIt is a fact or a topic, not an argument.Add should and an action that some people would resist.“AI is changing work.” → “Universities should teach AI literacy to every first-year student because…”
Who should do whatNo actor, or a vague action.Name one actor (a university, employers, a government, students) and one concrete action.“There should be more training.” → “Employers should pay for reskilling when automation changes a job because…”
Future of workThe topic is off course.Link it to jobs, skills, careers or workplaces.“Students should sleep more.” → “Companies should stop sending work messages after 8 p.m. because…”
Fits 4–5 minToo big to prove in one speech.Shrink it: one actor, one place, one action.“Governments should solve AI unemployment.” → “Companies that use AI to screen job applications should have a human review every rejection because…”

No “because” yet? Add your main reason last. It can change after you check your sources.

Classify a source by what it does. A report presenting its own data can be primary. A report summarizing other studies is secondary. AI chatbots are not sources.

  • I have at least one primary and one secondary source.
  • At least one source is from EAP 100 materials.
  • I checked every statistic and quotation in its original source.
