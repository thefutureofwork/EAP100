Part B · 10 points

11 Source types

Classify each source as primary or secondary, and explain why.
Source A: A report in which a research organization surveys workers and presents its own original survey findings.
Source B: A news article that explains and comments on the findings of Source A.

12 Arguable claim

The topic “AI in hiring” is not yet a claim. Rewrite it as one specific, arguable claim using the formula [Who] should [do what] because [main reason].

13 Persuasive appeal

Read this fictional example from a rehearsal.
“A study shows that many employers now use AI to screen job applications. This proves AI screening is unfair, so every company should stop using it.”
(a) Which appeal is the speaker mainly trying to use, and why? (b) Suggest one way to make this appeal stronger and more ethical.

14 Peer feedback

Your partner’s rehearsal lasts 7 minutes, excluding Q&A, and uses 6 content slides plus a references slide. Give two specific pieces of feedback based on the guide.

15 Deadline
