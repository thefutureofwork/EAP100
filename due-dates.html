⚠ Falling behind?

If you are more than one week behind, do not try to do everything at once. Triage:

  1. Current week's reading + listening task first. These feed the next class.
  2. Any graded assessment due this week or next. Exams, speeches, portfolio builds.
  3. Skip cosmetic tasks (time tracking, planning guide) and focus on substance.
  4. Email me at kt1992@nyu.edu with the subject “Catching up” and I will help you make a plan.
