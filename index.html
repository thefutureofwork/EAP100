Class Policies

Quick Reminders

  • ❓ Ask questions in the moment. Do not wait until the end of class. If something is unclear, raise your hand or speak up right away.
  • 🗣️ Check information with your instructor. You are welcome to speak to classmates, but do not assume what they tell you is correct. When in doubt, ask me directly.
  • 📅 Office hours: Walk-in on Tuesdays, 13:00–14:15 (Room N745). For other times, book an appointment at least 24 hours in advance.
  • 🤖 AI policy: Submitting AI-generated content as your own work is never allowed. When AI use is permitted on a task, you must include a disclosure statement. Your instructor will tell you when AI is permitted.

FAQ

Where do I submit my work?

Upload all assignments to your Student Folder on the course Google Drive. Use the week label from the assignment (e.g., "Week 1 Reading 1 Paragraph"). Keep a copy of portfolio-related work in your portfolio.

How do I book office hours?

Walk-in: Tuesdays, 13:00–14:15, Room N745. No appointment needed, first come first served. By appointment: Book here at least 24 hours in advance.

What if I miss a class?

You have 2 free absences (no explanation needed). For each absence, check with a classmate about what was covered. 3 absences = participation deduction. 4 = F for participation. 5 = course failure. If you know in advance, notify me before class.

What if my assignment is late?

5% deducted per day late. Not accepted after 7 days. If you have extenuating circumstances, email me before the due date. An absence does not excuse a late submission.

Can I use AI tools for assignments?

AI use is sometimes restricted and task-specific. Your instructor will tell you when AI is permitted. Submitting AI-generated content as your own work is never allowed. When AI use is permitted, you must include an AI disclosure statement. See the Syllabus for full details.

What if I miss a CCD visit?

Check the CCD calendar for alternative events, speak directly to the CCD (Room W309) to arrange attendance at another session, and then inform your instructor. All 4 visits and reflections are required.
